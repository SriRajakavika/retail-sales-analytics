# Retail Sales Analytics

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing retail sales, customer behavior, product performance, store performance, staff performance, and inventory levels.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze sales and revenue trends
- Identify top-performing products
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior
- Segment customers using RFM analysis
- Evaluate store and staff performance
- Identify low-stock products
- Build an interactive business dashboard

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Excel** – Data cleaning and preprocessing
- **MySQL** – Database management and SQL analysis
- **Python** – EDA and customer segmentation
- **Pandas** – Data manipulation
- **Matplotlib** – Data visualization
- **Power BI** – Interactive dashboard development

## 🔄 Project Workflow

**Data → Excel Cleaning → MySQL → SQL Analysis → Python EDA → RFM Segmentation → Power BI Dashboard → Business Insights**

## 📊 Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard includes:

- **Sales Overview** – Revenue, orders, sales trends and top products
- **Stores & Staffs** – Store sales, staff performance and low-stock monitoring
- **Customer Analysis** – City-wise revenue, customer segments and segment revenue

## 👥 Customer Segmentation

Customer segmentation was performed using **RFM Analysis**:

- **Recency** – How recently a customer purchased
- **Frequency** – How frequently a customer purchased
- **Monetary** – How much a customer spent

Customers were categorized into:

**Regular | Loyal | At Risk**

## 📈 Key Insights

- Total revenue reached approximately **8.58M**
- Revenue peaked in **2017**
- Trek models dominated the top-performing products
- Staff revenue performance varied across employees
- Products with stock below **30 units** were flagged for replenishment
- Regular customers contributed the largest share of segment revenue
- At-Risk customers require targeted retention strategies
